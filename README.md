## 👋 What Is This?

chat-on-steroids is a **bridge** that connects ChatGPT to your Windows computer. It lets ChatGPT help you with tasks on your own machine — like reading files, organizing folders, or even using your computer — but **only in the places you approve**. Think of it as giving ChatGPT a limited, supervised key to your computer.

You stay in control. ChatGPT can only access folders and abilities you explicitly allow. Nothing more.

## 🧰 Troubleshooting

### The app won't install
- Make sure you have Windows 10 or Windows 11
- Try right-clicking the installer and selecting "Run as administrator"
- Temporarily disable your antivirus, then try again

### ChatGPT won't connect
- Check your internet connection
- Make sure you're logged into ChatGPT in your browser
- Restart the app

### The app is not responding
- Close the app and reopen it
- If that doesn't work, restart your computer

### I can't see my folders
- Make sure you've granted access in the settings
- Check that the folder still exists on your computer
